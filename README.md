### :bust_in_silhouette: About Me

I am **Shree Hari S B**, an undergraduate Computer Science Engineering student at **Jain Institute of Technology, Davangere**, specializing in Artificial Intelligence, real-time Computer Vision pipelines, and production full-stack systems. My work focuses on building interactive, high-performance software—ranging from touchless camera-based hand gesture control with OpenCV and MediaPipe to 60 FPS 3D WebGL simulations and cloud-deployed web applications on Render and Vercel. I engineer software with clean architecture, robust algorithms, and practical utility, and I am actively seeking software engineering internships, technical collaborations, and open-source contributions.
